Donation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Acceptable Donations & Minimum Gift

Mustaqiim Relief and Charity Foundation accepts voluntary monetary contributions starting at US$3 (or equivalent in Ugandan Shillings or accepted currencies) to support humanitarian relief, food parcels, clean water wells, and Qurbani programmes. We reserve the right to decline contributions that conflict with our charitable purpose or applicable anti-money-laundering laws.

2. Programme Designation

Donors may designate their contribution to a specific campaign (such as Qurbani 2026, Ramadan Food Packs, or Clean Water Wells) or to General Humanitarian Relief. Designated funds are applied strictly to the chosen programme. If a specific campaign reaches full funding or cannot proceed due to local conditions, remaining funds are allocated to an equivalent humanitarian need after notifying donors where reachable.

3. Qurbani / Udhiya Standards

Qurbani livestock are inspected for health and Islamic eligibility, slaughtered during the prescribed days of Eid al-Adha, and distributed as fresh meat to vulnerable households.

4. Receipts & Accountability

Every direct transfer coordinated through our Kampala office receives a written confirmation reference. Only verified, audited figures are published as official impact statistics.

5. Refunds

See our dedicated Refund Policy for eligibility and 14-day review procedures.
