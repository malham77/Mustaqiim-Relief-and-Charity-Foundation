Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Because donations to Mustaqiim Relief and Charity Foundation are immediately allocated to humanitarian relief, food procurement, and community projects, charitable contributions are generally non-refundable once disbursed. However, we treat donor trust (Amanah) seriously and honor verified refund requests in the situations below.

1. Eligible Refund Scenarios

  • Duplicate Transfers: If you accidentally sent the same donation twice.
  • Technical or Entry Error: If an incorrect amount was transferred due to a technical fault or clerical mistake.
  • Unauthorized Transaction: If a transfer was made without the account holder's authorization.
  • Unfulfilled Qurbani Reservation: If you cancel a Qurbani reservation before livestock procurement or slaughter has taken place, or if we are unable to fulfill a confirmed reservation.

2. Request Timeframe

Please submit your refund request within 14 calendar days of the transaction date (or prior to Eid al-Adha livestock procurement for Qurbani bookings).

3. How to Request a Refund

Contact our Kampala coordination team via our Contact page or WhatsApp (+256 708 684 223) with your full name, transfer date, amount, payment reference, and reason for the request.

4. Verification & Processing

Approved refunds are returned using the original transfer method whenever possible within 7 to 14 business days after verification. Any third-party bank or mobile money network fees already deducted by the payment carrier may affect the net refundable amount.
