Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Mustaqiim Relief and Charity Foundation respects your privacy and minimizes browser storage. We do not use advertising cookies, behavioral profiling, or cross-site tracking networks.

1. Categories of Storage We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Storage: Used to remember your cookie consent selection (`mq_cookie_prefs`) and maintain secure authentication sessions for authorized administrators in the Admin Dashboard.
  • Functional Map Tiles (Optional): On our About page, interactive map tiles from OpenStreetMap may set standard network cache headers when loaded.

2. Managing or Clearing Your Preferences

You can update your cookie preferences at any time using the button below or via the "Manage Cookie Preferences" link in our website footer, or clear local browser data from our Data Deletion page.
