Qurbani / Udhiya

Share the blessings of Eid al-Adha

Each Qurbani share is sourced locally, slaughtered according to Islamic principles, and distributed fresh to families, widows, and orphans who would otherwise go without.

How Qurbani Coordination Works

  1. Select your animal and share preference using the estimator below.
  2. Message our Kampala team on WhatsApp to confirm live seasonal pricing.
  3. We procure healthy livestock from Ugandan farmers and perform slaughter during the blessed days of Eid al-Adha.
  4. Fresh meat portions are distributed directly to verified vulnerable households across Kampala and rural districts.
