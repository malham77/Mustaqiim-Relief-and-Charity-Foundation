About Us

A foundation built on trust, dignity, and direct community service.

Coordinating humanitarian aid, child education care, food parcels, clean water projects, and Qurbani from Nsambya, Kampala across Uganda.

Our Mission

To relieve hardship and support vulnerable families and children through humanitarian aid, educational supplies, Qurbani, food security, clean water access, and community-driven charity delivered with integrity and accountability.

Our Vision

A society where every family and child has access to nutrition, safe water, learning opportunities, and the daily essentials of a dignified life.

How Our Nsambya Team Operates

Before launching any local distribution, our Kampala field volunteers collaborate with village elders, community health workers, and school administrators to verify household needs quietly and accurately. Direct delivery prevents unnecessary administrative delays and upholds personal privacy for widows, elderly residents, and young learners.

Core Principles

  • Amanah (Trust)

    Every contribution is handled carefully and allocated to its designated programme.

  • Dignity & Child Safeguarding

    We protect the privacy, safety, and dignity of every household and child we serve.

  • Community Partnership

    Local leaders, teachers, and volunteers help guide distribution priorities.

  • Data Minimization

    We collect only necessary contact information and never sell donor data.
