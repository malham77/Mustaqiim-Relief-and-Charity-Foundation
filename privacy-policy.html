Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Mustaqiim Relief and Charity Foundation ("we", "our", "the Foundation"), operating from Nsambya, Kampala, Uganda, is committed to safeguarding your personal information in accordance with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and internationally recognized privacy principles.

1. Data Minimization — We Collect Only Needed Data

We strictly avoid unnecessary data collection. You can browse this website, read about our campaigns, and view programme updates without creating an account or submitting personal data.

  • Contact Inquiries: Only your name, one reply channel (phone number or email), topic, and message.
  • Donation Coordination: No payment card numbers or sensitive financial credentials are collected or stored on this website. Donation pledges are coordinated directly via WhatsApp or official channels.
  • Data Requests: Only the identifying details required to locate and erase or export your record.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to programme, Qurbani, volunteer, or donor inquiries.
  • To allocate designated donations accurately and provide acknowledgement receipts.
  • To process data access, correction, or deletion requests.

3. Children's Privacy & Age Consent

Our online forms and donation coordination services are intended for individuals aged 18 years or older. If a person under 18 wishes to submit an inquiry or participate in charitable giving, verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian is required. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental authorization. If you believe a minor has submitted data without consent, please request immediate erasure via our Data Deletion Request page.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Limited technical processing occurs with:

  • Cloud Database Hosting (Google Firebase): Stores public website content and encrypted inquiry/deletion submissions with strict access controls restricted to authorized Foundation administrators.
  • WhatsApp: If you choose to message us via WhatsApp, your communication is governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap: Only loaded if permitted in your Cookie Preferences on the About page.

5. Data Retention & Security

Inquiry records are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your request or satisfy statutory accounting obligations in Uganda. Administrative access is protected by verified Google Authentication and role-based database rules.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal data at any time. Submit a request directly on our Data Deletion Request page.

7. Privacy Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact our Kampala coordination office via WhatsApp (+256 708 684 223) or submit a request on our Data Deletion page.
